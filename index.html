Level 3 · Predictive & Prescriptive

What will happen, and what to do

Six modules, each with an expandable methodology & limitations note: forecast method, assumptions, model drift risk, and the correlation-not-causation caveat — directly addressing the brief's predictive-capability requirement.

Module 1

UK Revenue Forecast

6 months forward · 80% confidence band · fan chart. Method: trend + leading-indicator regression on UK consumer confidence, FX, and prior-period attach rate.

Module 2

Attrition Risk Score by Segment

Tenure × role × location matrix. Highest-risk employees flagged with RAG. Tooltip cites Davenport, Harris & Shapiro (2010) on workforce-analytics maturity.

Module 3

Wage Inflation Margin Impact Simulator

Slider 0–10% UK wage inflation → live UK margin readout. Inputs: labour cost share of revenue, services attach rate, sterling FX assumption.

Module 4

Samsung Product Launch Impact Model

Predicted UK market share shift on Galaxy AI launch. Calibration: prior six Samsung launches, post-launch share movement, Counterpoint UK panel data.

Module 5

Regulatory Change Cost Scenarios

DMA App Store compliance — three scenarios: minimal compliance · moderate · full third-party app store opening. Cost outputs: revenue at risk, engineering investment, regulator-relations overhead.

Module 6

Scenario Sandbox

Leadership sets assumptions (FX, wage inflation, Services growth) and sees forward-looking outcomes. Designed for pre-board off-site use.
