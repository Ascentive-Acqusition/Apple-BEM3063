Section 01

Purpose & design intent

This dashboard exists to give the Apple UK leadership team integrated visibility over the firm's strategic and HR position in real time, without requiring them to assemble briefings from disparate functional reports.

The brief that initiated this build was direct: several Apple UK business units are under-performing key rivals, no real-time monitoring system currently exists, and the new CEO needs an instrument that captures both day-to-day operating signals and forward-looking predictive capability. This dashboard answers that brief.

The design follows the seven Topic 8 "Good Dashboard" principles taught in BEM3063: clear and relevant metrics, customisation and flexibility, real-time data, data with instruction on how to use it, benchmarking and historical comparison, filtering / interpretation / consolidation, and predictive capability. Every design decision below traces back to one of these principles.

i
This dashboard is a decision-support instrument, not a single source of truth. Every figure is anchored to a publicly auditable source where one exists, and clearly labelled "illustrative" where one does not. The User Guide treats this distinction as load-bearing.

Section 03

Reading a tile

Every Level 1 tile follows the same anatomy. Once the convention is learned, any tile can be read in five seconds.

Tile anatomy — top to bottom
  1. Label (top-left) — the metric name, written in plain language. Re-labelled per role.
  2. RAG dot (top-right) — single colour-coded indicator: green within target band, amber on watch, red critical.
  3. Value (large, centre) — the headline number. Updates in near-real-time on the live data feed.
  4. Sub-value — the comparator: a target, a benchmark, or the rival figure, so the headline is never decontextualised.
  5. Delta pill — directional change vs prior period, with an arrow (↑ ↓ →) and a coloured tint.
  6. Visualisation — sparkline, donut, gauge, or comparison bar, depending on what the metric needs.
  7. Source citation (footer) — the exact data origin and currency, so any number can be audited back to filings.
  8. Updated timestamp — when the figure was last refreshed.

Click or tap any tile (or press Enter when focused) to open the slide-over drill-down sheet — see Section 07 for what that sheet contains.

Section 04

RAG thresholds & how they are set

RAG (Red · Amber · Green) status follows defined rules. Status is not subjective — it is set by the variance against a stated target or benchmark, with override criteria for leading-indicator drift.

StatusDefault ruleOverride conditions
Green Within ±5% of target / benchmark Demoted to amber if a leading indicator (e.g. a cohort breakdown) is materially worse, even where the headline is on target.
Amber 5–15% variance from target, OR upward-trending risk Escalated to red if the variance widens for two consecutive periods, or if a critical alert is open against the same business unit.
Red >15% variance, OR an active critical alert exists No override — red triggers SLA-clock visibility for the named owner.
!
Why some tiles look "harsher" than the headline number suggests. Engagement at 72/100 against a 75 target is mathematically only −4% — green by the default rule. It is shown as amber because the trend is downward across three consecutive quarters and the retail-floor cohort has fallen to 68. The dashboard prefers to surface a softening signal early rather than mask it inside a band-tolerance that is still nominally green.
Section 05

Filters & how they compose

Four filter dimensions — Period, Business Unit, Geography, Cohort — mutate the dashboard's data in real time. Filters compose: applying multiple at once narrows the view conjunctively.

FilterOptionsWhat changes
PeriodQ · YTD · 12M · CustomTime horizon for every metric. Period-sensitive figures (revenue, attrition rate) recompute; structural figures (gender pay gap, regulatory cases) don't.
Business UnitAll · Retail · Services · Corporate / B2B · OnlineNarrows revenue and customer figures to the chosen unit. Selecting Services amplifies the under-performance signal because the Services view isolates the gap from US growth.
GeographyUK · England · Scotland · Wales · NIRe-scopes geo-sensitive metrics. Scotland, for example, has a narrower store-productivity spread because no London flagships sit inside it.
CohortAll staff · Retail floor · Genius · Corporate HQ · Online support · LeadershipRe-scopes people metrics. The retail-floor cohort surfaces 12.1% attrition and 68 engagement — the leading-indicator view of the headline 8.4% / 72.

Active filters are highlighted in blue on the chip bar. Click Reset at the right of the filter bar to return to the default 12M / All / UK / All staff view.

→
How filters compose. Period × BU × Geo × Cohort combine conjunctively — selecting "Q" + "Retail" + "England" + "Retail floor" gives quarter-only data for retail-floor employees in English Apple Retail UK Ltd stores. Where data is too thin to support a combination, the affected tile shows "—" rather than fabricating a number.
Section 06

Role views

The role pill in the top-right swaps both the order of the twelve tiles and the emphasis of selected labels and sub-values. Same data, four lenses.

CEO view

Default. Foregrounds revenue, services growth, engagement, alerts, and BU portfolio status. Designed for the daily integrative scan across strategic, financial, operational, and HR signals.

CFO view

Foregrounds £-denominated and disclosure-risk metrics. Tile 1 sub-value flips from "Target £3.30bn FY26" to "−£160m gap"; pay-gap tile re-labels as "Pay equity · disclosure risk."

COO view

Foregrounds operational levers. Store-productivity tile rises to position one; revenue tile reframes as "Channel revenue throughput" with the Retail/Online split surfaced.

CHRO view

Foregrounds people metrics. Engagement and attrition rise to positions one and two. Revenue tile recomputes as "Revenue per UK head" (~£525k / FTE). Early-tenure cohort callouts are made first-class.

The role view is purely a presentation choice — the underlying data does not change. A CFO and a CHRO viewing the same period under the same filters will see the same figures, just emphasised differently.

Section 07

The drill-down sheet

Clicking any tile opens a slide-over panel from the right. The sheet contains everything needed to interrogate the figure, including the Companies House proxy disclosures and any active alerts attached to that metric.

  1. Hero value & sub-value — the same headline restated at large size for share-screen reading.
  2. RAG with rationale — the explicit reason for the current status, not just the colour.
  3. Trend chart — the metric's history at a larger scale than the tile sparkline allows.
  4. Narrative — one paragraph explaining why this metric matters for the under-performing-units diagnosis.
  5. Breakdown table — the constituent figures, including any data-quality flags ("Excluded" rows, audit currency).
  6. Active alerts / segments — for tiles that aggregate (alerts, BU RAG, regulatory), each underlying line is listed with its owner.
  7. Source citation — the auditable origin (e.g. Apple (UK) Ltd, Companies House 01591116, filing of 27 Sep 2025).

Close the sheet with the ✕ button, by clicking outside it, or by pressing Escape.

Section 08

Predictive modules (Level 3)

Level 3 contains six interactive modules. Each module is paired with a methodology & limitations drawer at the bottom — a deliberate design choice, since predictive output without stated assumptions is decision-grade misinformation.

ModuleWhat it predictsMethodKey limitation
1 · Revenue ForecastUK revenue 3 / 6 / 12 months forwardOLS regression, lagged consumer-confidence + £/$ inputs, R² ≈ 0.71Structural break risk on Galaxy AI launch
2 · Attrition Risk Matrix12-month departure probability by tenure × roleLogistic regression on tenure, role, location, manager ratingPredictive ≠ prescriptive without intervention design (Davenport, Harris & Shapiro, 2010)
3 · Wage Inflation SimulatorUK margin under different wage / attach scenariosElasticity model: margin = base − β·Δwage + γ·ΔattachHolds revenue and FX constant; no price pass-through
4 · Galaxy S26 ImpactiOS share movement post-launchCalibrated on prior six Samsung launches, scaled by feature-overlapExcludes Pixel 10 cross-pressure
5 · DMA Compliance ScenariosUK revenue at risk under three regulatory statesEU DMA cost as proxy, scaled by UK App Store revenue shareExcludes brand-equity impact
6 · Scenario SandboxComposite revenue and margin under FX × wage × Services-growth inputsMultiplicative compositeHolds competitor moves constant
!
Read the methodology drawer before quoting any L3 figure in a board paper. The forecasts are conditional projections, not guarantees. Correlation is not causation, and historical fits do not guarantee future performance — particularly through structural breaks like a major rival's AI launch.

i
Why UK revenue is shown as £3.14bn. Apple's full UK economic activity is materially larger than the sum of the two UK-incorporated entities, but it is routed through Apple Distribution International (Ireland) and is not separately disclosed at country level. The £3.14bn figure used here is the most defensible publicly auditable UK anchor — not a claim of total UK sales. The Tile 1 drill-down sheet labels this explicitly under "Excluded".
Section 10

Decision workflows

Three end-to-end workflows show how the dashboard is intended to be used in live decision-making. Each follows the descriptive → diagnostic → predictive arc.

Workflow A · Daily executive scan (60 seconds)
1
Open Level 1 with default filters
Twelve tiles render in 60 seconds. Scan the RAG indicators left-to-right.
2
Click any red or newly-amber tile
Read the rationale, the narrative, and the source. Note the owner if alerts are attached.
3
Decide whether to escalate
If escalation is warranted, the alert SLA clock begins from the moment the tile is opened.
Workflow B · Diagnosing UK Services under-performance
1
L1 → confirm the headline
Tile 3 shows UK Services growing at +6.4% YoY against US Services at +12.1% — a 5.7pp gap.
2
L2 → trace the cause through the Balanced Scorecard
The chevron at the top of L2 names the bottleneck: Internal Processes → Customer. Customer panel shows 2.4× attach-rate dispersion across stores. Learning & Growth panel shows 18.2% <12-month-tenure attrition.
3
L3 → quantify the lever
Module 3 (wage / attach simulator): a 2pp Services-attach uplift recovers ~0.4pp of operating margin. Module 6 (sandbox): the same uplift adds ~£90m to FY26 revenue at constant FX.
Workflow C · Pre-board off-site planning
1
Set scenarios in L3 Module 6
Use the FX × wage × Services-growth sliders to test bull, base, and bear cases.
2
Pair with Module 5
Toggle between Minimal / Moderate / Full opening DMA scenarios to layer regulatory cost on top of the macro outlook.
3
Read the methodology drawers
Quote any L3 figure in board papers only with its stated R², its assumptions, and its limitations. The drawers exist for exactly this purpose.

Section 12

Glossary

Selected terms used across the dashboard, defined in the sense intended in BEM3063 and Apple's UK reporting context.

TermDefinition
Attach rateProportion of hardware purchases that result in a Services subscription within a defined window. The UK / US gap in this metric drives the under-performing-units diagnosis.
Balanced ScorecardA four-lens performance framework (Kaplan & Norton, 2005) that links Learning & Growth → Internal Processes → Customer → Financial outcomes as a causal chain.
BSC bottleneckThe point in the causal chain at which the firm's performance breaks. Apple UK's bottleneck is between Internal Processes and Customer (the staff-to-Services translation gap).
DMAUK Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act. Creates one-way regulatory asymmetry in the UK relative to the US.
Glassdoor proxyUse of public Glassdoor UK ratings as a triangulating signal alongside the internal pulse-survey composite.
Leading indicatorA metric that moves before the headline figure does. The retail-floor cohort engagement at 68 is a leading indicator of the headline 72 falling further.
RAGRed · Amber · Green status indicator. See Section 04 for the threshold rules.
SLA clockService Level Agreement timer. Begins the moment a critical alert is opened, and tracks against the named owner's resolution commitment.
Two-entity proxyThe disclosed-turnover figure built from Apple (UK) Ltd + Apple Retail UK Ltd. Excludes UK sales routed through Apple Distribution International (Ireland) — see Section 09.
